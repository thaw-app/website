# antislop audit 002, 2026-10-06

Scope: the site as it stands after the rebuild of the home page (the README
as the right column), the changelog timeline, the roadmap board, the
community page, `/try` and the footer. Run with antislop-ui, antislop-human,
antislop-layoutmobile and antislop-copywriting. The owner asked for the
findings to be fixed as part of the run, so each line says what was done.

Measured in a headless browser at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, with touch
emulation for tap targets and real Tab presses for focus.

## Fixed

1. **Keyboard focus was left to the browser's default ring** (R-32, R-34),
   which is dark blue in some browsers and close to invisible on black. One
   ring for the whole site now: 2px in the text colour, offset 2px. 17.9:1 on
   the dark page, 18.2:1 on the light one.
2. **Touch targets under 44px** (R-03): contributor tags (34px), release tags
   on the changelog (28px), the macOS filter buttons (34px), the install tabs
   and Copy, the footer's links (16 to 19px), the programme badges (24px) and
   the community page's join buttons (42px). All grow to 44px on a touch
   screen and are unchanged with a mouse.
3. **The changelog's calendar was clipped on a phone**: it kept its 14px
   squares and hid what did not fit. Its squares now shrink to fit: 358px
   wide at a 390px screen, nothing cut off.
4. **A day with one release was 2.4:1 against the page** (non-text contrast).
   The faintest shade is now 6.0:1 in dark and 3.2:1 in light.
5. **The conure washed out on a light page**: yellow at 1.5:1, green at
   2.9:1. It has darker tones in light mode now (5.1, 4.6 and 6.0:1). It is
   decoration, but it should be seen.
6. **The cube filled the first screen on a phone and a tablet**, pushing the
   headline and Install down. It is smaller below the two-column width.
7. **The community page's intro hid who does the work** in a passive
   sentence. It now reads: "The people who use it report its bugs, write its
   fixes and translate it."
8. **The home page's section strip overflowed at 1024px**, cutting off Floe.
   Its tabs are tighter below the widest layout; it still scrolls sideways if
   it must.

## Checked and left

- **No sideways scroll** on any page at any of the four widths.
- **No em dashes** in text written for this site.
- **Every number on the community page is fetched**, with its source in the
  caption and the date it was counted (R-17).
- **The header's own search and menu buttons are 32 to 34px** on a phone.
  They are Fumadocs' and were left as they come.
- **Inline links inside sentences** (Raycast and Droppy in the README's
  list) are text-height, as links in prose are.
- **Two things never stop moving**: the language strip and the conure. Both
  were asked for, both stop when off screen or under the pointer, and both
  are still with reduced motion (R-19, with the owner's reason).
- **Docs text runs the full width on a very wide window**, which makes long
  lines. The owner asked for the page to reach the right edge.
- **The page's shape is still Better Auth's**, by the owner's choice (R-30).
