team, contributors, Take part), the Built with page, the site-map footer, the

`/built-with`, a missing address, the roadmap, the changelog): text contrast measured
on every text node in both themes at 1440 wide, overflow and tap targets at 390 wide
with touch on, and the console read throughout.
