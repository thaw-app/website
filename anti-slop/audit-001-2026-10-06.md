# antislop audit 001, 2026-10-06

Scope: the home page (`app/(home)/page.tsx`), the mock desktop
(`components/desktop/`), and the docs pages written in this repo. Mode 2:
audit after the fact. Nothing here has been changed; pick the numbers to fix.

Design Read: a product home page for Mac users choosing a menu bar manager,
in a hairline-and-monospace language borrowed from Better Auth at the owner's
request, dials ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM,
Quality Lock = LOW.

## HIGH

1. **Controls in the mock that do nothing** (R-26). The toolbar's sidebar,
   back, forward and search controls are inert. Push buttons in the panes
   (Record Shortcut, Open Layout, Identify, Check Now and the rest) press but
   do nothing. In the Thaw menu and the "⋯" menu, most rows only close the
   menu. The caption says the desktop is a rebuild, but no control is
   labelled as inactive.
2. **The mock is not usable on a phone** (R-03). It scales to about 36% at
   390 px wide, so switches and sidebar rows are far under the 44 px tap
   target and the text is unreadable. It works as a picture there, not as a
   demo.
3. **Dragging has no keyboard path** (R-32). Reordering items in the Layout
   pane and Command-dragging in the menu bar are pointer only. Items in the
   menu bar other than Thaw's own cannot be focused at all.
4. **Contrast not measured** (R-25). Muted text on the new black background,
   the uppercase labels at 12 px, and the fainter tones of the ASCII cube
   have not been checked against 4.5:1. The cube is decorative, the labels
   are not.
5. **No written design direction** (R-37). There is no `DESIGN.md`. Direction
   has come from the owner in conversation (Better Auth's structure, the
   logo's orange, Innovator Grotesk, true black). It should be written down
   so later work has something to be held to.

## MEDIUM

6. **Uppercase monospace labels everywhere** (R-06). Section names, the tab
   strip, both buttons, the licence line and the footer are all wide-tracked
   uppercase mono. It is the look of the reference site, and it is also the
   most recognisable "technical landing page" tell. Reason on file: owner
   asked for the Better Auth look. Worth deciding how much of it to keep.
7. **Arrows on most links** (R-08). "Read the docs →", "Everything else is in
   the docs →", "Floe docs →", and "↗" on two footer links. Only the external
   links have a reason for theirs.
8. **One icon library throughout** (R-04). Every icon outside the mock is
   Lucide: the download, arrow and copy icons, every docs sidebar icon and
   every docs card icon. Relevant, but uniform.
9. **Nine identical feature cells** (R-14). Same size, same structure, no
   hierarchy, although "hide what you don't need" and "made for the notch"
   are the product and "20 languages" is a footnote.
10. **Numbers 01 to 09 on the features** (R-05, R-31). The features are not a
    sequence, so the numbers carry no information.
11. **Purple in the mock's wallpaper** (R-01). The wallpaper blends orange
    into blue through a purple bloom. Orange and blue are the two products;
    the purple has no source and is the stock gradient colour.
12. **The ASCII cube animates forever** (R-19). It never settles. Purpose on
    file: it is the page's identity piece. It could run on load and on
    hover and rest in between.

## LOW

13. **The page's structure is Better Auth's** (R-30). Split columns, tab
    strip, numbered grid. The owner asked for it, so this is recorded as an
    override, not a defect.
14. **The docs are stock Fumadocs** (R-20). Apart from the icons and the
    product accent colour they would look the same under any logo.
15. **Three radius languages on one page** (R-11). Square buttons and boxes
    on the home page, Fumadocs' rounded controls in the header and docs, and
    macOS radii inside the mock. The mock's are correct; the other two could
    agree.
16. **Two spellings of the Claude programme** (R-31). The badge reads
    "Claude Open Source Program"; its link goes to a page titled "Claude for
    Open Source".

## Passed

- No em dashes in text written for this site (R-02).
- No invented statistics, testimonials or logos (R-17, R-18, R-36, R-38).
  "20 languages" and every feature line come from the README; "Works with"
  links to real integrations.
- Every navigation link has a destination (R-24): checked by request.
- Light and dark both render (R-34): checked by screenshot.
- Buttons name their action (R-15); no buzzwords (R-16).
- The theme is the visitor's choice, with a working toggle (R-21).
