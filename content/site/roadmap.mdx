Thaw has two lines in development: 3.0 for macOS 27 and 2.1 for macOS 26. This is the plan as it stands, not a promise, and your reports decide the order. It was last brought up to date with the [3.0.0-beta.2 notes](/docs/thaw/changelog/3.0.0/3.0.0-beta.2) of 6 October 2026.

<Callout type="info" title="macOS 15 is coming back">
  We are adding compatibility with macOS 15 back in Thaw 2.2.0.
</Callout>

<Roadmap>

<RoadmapList title="Now" count={2} state="now" note="The two releases being finished.">
<RoadmapGroup name="macOS 27" count={1}>

- **Thaw 3.0.0.** In beta. It is a rebuilt menu bar core for macOS 27, and the interface is being trimmed and tidied over the next betas.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="macOS 26" count={1}>

- **Thaw 2.1.0.** At its [first release candidate](/docs/thaw/changelog/2.1.0/2.1.0-rc.1). It ships as 2.1.0 if no new problems turn up.

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Up next" count={7} state="next" note="Planned for the 3.0.0 betas.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Settings" count={1}>

- **Fewer switches.** Every setting is being gone through: some become default behaviour, some move under Advanced, and some go away. Experiments either graduate or get removed. The first pass shipped in 3.0.0-beta.1.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Layout" count={2}>

- **Stand-ins without a switch.** Dragging an Apple item Thaw can’t hide into Hidden should put a Thaw icon there for you. Today that takes a switch in Experiments.
- **Undo for drags.** Command-Z will undo mouse moves in Layout, the way it already undoes keyboard moves.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Coming over from 2.1" count={4}>

- **Scripts.** Script-driven bar modules, tested by macOS 26 users on 2.1.0, are the next thing coming to 3.0. The Scripts pane is already there as a preview.
- **The full trigger engine.** In 2.1 an item can move on battery level, the frontmost app, a network, a Focus and more. 3.0 has part of it: 3.0.0-beta.2 added showing items while an app runs.
- **Widgets.** The Widgets pane is a placeholder so the destination is discoverable. It holds no settings yet.
- **Rotating diagnostic logs.** Keeping and cycling through diagnostic logs will appear in a later 3.0 build.

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Blocked by macOS 27" count={4} state="blocked" note="These run into limits in macOS 27 itself. The team is looking for ways around them and can’t promise them for the beta.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Hiding" count={2}>

- **Separate items from the same app.** macOS 27 hides and shows an app’s items together, so one of an app’s items can’t yet go in Hidden while another stays in Visible. <Issue n={1125} />
- **The Shortcuts item.** macOS 27 can hide it at launch, and Thaw can’t bring it back yet. <Issue n={1154} />

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Layout" count={2}>

- **Even spacing.** Each app adds its own padding around its items, so the same spacing setting can still leave uneven gaps. <Issue n={1126} />
- **A Timer stand-in.** Thaw can’t remove Apple’s Timer, so it can’t put one of its own in its place.

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Further out" count={6} state="later" note="These come after the beta is solid. The team intends to build them, and none has a date.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Integrations" count={2}>

- **An Alfred workflow.** One you can install, on top of the `thaw://` links that [already work with Alfred](/docs/thaw/uri-schemes).
- **Import from another menu bar manager.** Bring your setup with you, so switching to Thaw doesn’t mean starting over.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Installing" count={2}>

- **Modules.** Download only the parts of Thaw you use, and add others when you want them, so the app you install is no bigger than what you need.
- **Nix support.** A flake, so Thaw can be installed and pinned from a Nix configuration.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Across your Macs" count={1}>

- **iCloud sync.** For profiles, appearance and preferences. Anything tied to one Mac’s displays stays local.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Menu bar items" count={1}>

- **Isolation mode.** Show just one item for a while, with everything else out of the way. <Issue n={970} />

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>
