ThawUI is the set of controls, colours, type and motion that Thaw and Floe are built from. Its code is moving to [its own repository](https://github.com/thaw-app/ThawUI) from [Thaw’s](https://github.com/thaw-app/Thaw/tree/development/ThawUI), and Floe keeps a copy of it for now. This is the plan as it stands, not a promise.

<Roadmap>

<RoadmapList title="Now" count={3} state="now" here note="What is being built, for the next releases of Thaw and Floe.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Thaw" count={2}>

- **A new design.** Settings is being made more like macOS’s own apps. Since 3.0.0-alpha.13 it is built on ThawUI, with a sidebar that puts the everyday pages first, and long pages show one tab at a time. <Marks of="thaw" />
- **Fewer switches.** Every setting is being gone through: some become default behaviour and some go away, and panes are merged. Experiments either graduate or get removed. It began in 3.0.0-alpha.4, which removed the Advanced pane. 3.0.0-beta.3 removed Thaw Bar Only and two Troubleshooting buttons, 3.0.0-alpha.11 turned two switches into one Hidden items picker, and 3.0.0-alpha.13 retired the Experiments pane, putting its features on their own pages with a badge. 2.2.0-beta.1 and 2.2.0-beta.2 merged pages and settings in Thaw 2. <Marks of="thaw" />

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Thaw and Floe" count={1}>

- **One look across both apps.** Their interfaces are being brought together on the same shared components, so a control looks and works the same in each. <Marks of="thaw floe" />

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>
