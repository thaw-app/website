Floe is early: its latest release is 0.2.1. This is the plan as it stands, not a promise, and the order can change. [What Floe does not do yet](/docs/floe/limitations) lists the gaps as they are today, and [ThawUI’s roadmap](/roadmap/thawui) has the design Floe shares with Thaw.

<Roadmap>

<RoadmapList title="Up next" count={3} state="now" here note="What comes next, in this order.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Documentation" count={1}>

- **A guide for extension authors.** How to run a local extension in Floe, what works and what does not.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Extensions" count={2}>

- **Floe’s own sign-in service.** Extensions sign in through Raycast’s service today, which is temporary. Floe’s own takes its place.
- **Deeplinks from outside Floe.** A `raycast://extensions/…` link opened in a browser or another app goes to Raycast today. Opened by an extension, it already runs in Floe.

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Later" count={3} state="later" note="These come after the list above. None has a date.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Extensions" count={1}>

- **An `@floe/api` package.** A small one, for what Raycast’s API cannot express.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Search" count={1}>

- **Search inside your notes.** Browse Notes finds a note by its name today.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Built in" count={1}>

- **Calculator history.**

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Not planned" count={1} state="blocked" note="Not on the plan unless people ask for it.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Windows" count={1}>

- **Window management.** There are many window managers already, and Floe can work with them.

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Shipped" count={41} state="done" note="Everything that has shipped, by release, newest first. The changelog has the detail.">

- Each AI service keeps its own API key

</RoadmapGroup>

<RoadmapGroup name="0.1.0" count={38}>

- One hotkey opens a search over applications, commands, quicklinks and System Settings
- Raycast extensions, unmodified, with an Extension Store page
- Aliases, hotkeys and favorites
- Shell commands, saved commands and script commands
- Checkpoints, and receipts that undo what Floe changed
- Reminders, calendar events and timers
- Snippets, quicklinks, emoji and a calculator with unit conversion
- File search and menu bar item search
- Search the commands in the front app’s menus
- A window switcher
- Bookmarks from Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Vivaldi, Dia and Helium
- The files you used lately in an app
- Quick Look on a file
- Answers to copy: the time in another city, a uuid, a password, placeholder text and text changes
- The Extension Store and Quit Floe, found in the search
- A clipboard history that keeps formatting, and leaves out what a password manager marks private
- Notes from a folder, read and written in Floe
- Translate, QR codes, places in Maps, sound devices, Homebrew, and your Mac’s battery, disk, memory and IP
- A colour or text picked from the screen
- Preferred apps: your terminal, editor, browser, notes app and clipboard manager
- Ask AI, answered by a tool or model you choose
- Thaw 3’s actions in the search
- Settings export and import
- Settings and passwords sync through iCloud, if you switch it on
- Scopes that narrow a search to one place
- Open browser tabs in the search
- A Fallbacks page for what is offered under every search
- `Floe --pick` lends the search panel to a script
- Contacts, and mail and message drafts
- Daily notes, with `todo` and `log`
- A Floating Note that stays above other apps
- Media controls and Focus
- Quit a process, and see what is on a port
- SSH hosts in the search
- A dictionary, from the ones on your Mac
- Apple Shortcuts in the search, and Floe’s actions in Shortcuts
- Each extension’s page shows what it reached
- Glass, a tint, a border, a shadow and a beam for the launcher

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>
