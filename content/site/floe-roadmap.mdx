Floe is early: 0.1.0 is its first release. This is the plan as it stands, not a promise, and the order can change. [What Floe does not do yet](/docs/floe/limitations) lists the gaps as they are today.

<Roadmap>

<RoadmapList title="Coming next" count={6} state="now" here note="Built since 0.1.0, and in the release after it.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Search" count={4}>

- **A window switcher.** Type windows and a title to switch to an open window, minimise it or close it.
- **Bookmarks.** Type bookmarks to search the bookmarks of Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Vivaldi, Dia and Helium.
- **Recent files of an app.** Tab on an app lists the files you used lately that open in it.
- **More found by typing.** The Extension Store, and Quit Floe.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Built in" count={2}>

- **Answers to copy.** The time in another city, a uuid, a password, placeholder text, and text turned into base64, a URL, upper or lower case or a SHA-256.
- **Quick Look.** Command-Y on a file shows it.

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Up next" count={5} state="next" note="What comes after that, in this order.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Documentation" count={1}>

- **A guide for extension authors.** How to run a local extension in Floe, what works and what does not.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Extensions" count={2}>

- **Floe’s own sign-in service.** Extensions sign in through Raycast’s service today, which is temporary. Floe’s own takes its place.
- **More of the extension API.** The grid layout, `launchCommand` and deeplinks.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Search" count={1}>

- **Search the front app’s menus.** Built on the menu scanner in [CMD-Z](https://github.com/stonerl/CMD-Z) and shared with Thaw.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Across your Macs" count={1}>

- **Settings sync switched on.** Releases signed for iCloud, then the same sync in Thaw. <Marks of="icloud" />

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Later" count={3} state="later" note="These come after the list above. None has a date.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Extensions" count={1}>

- **An `@floe/api` package.** A small one, for what Raycast’s API cannot express.

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Search" count={1}>

- **Search your notes.**

</RoadmapGroup>
<RoadmapGroup name="Built in" count={1}>

- **Calculator history.**

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Not planned" count={1} state="blocked" note="Not on the plan unless people ask for it.">
<RoadmapGroup name="Windows" count={1}>

- **Window management.** There are many window managers already, and Floe can work with them.

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

<RoadmapList title="Shipped" count={12} state="done" note="Everything that has shipped, by release, newest first. The changelog has the detail.">
<RoadmapGroup name="0.1.0" count={12}>

- One hotkey opens a search over applications, commands, quicklinks and System Settings
- Raycast extensions, unmodified, with an Extension Store page
- Aliases, hotkeys and favorites
- Shell commands, saved commands and script commands
- Checkpoints, and receipts that undo what Floe changed
- Reminders, calendar events and timers
- Snippets, quicklinks, emoji and a calculator with unit conversion
- File search and menu bar item search
- Preferred apps: your terminal, editor, browser, notes app and clipboard manager
- Ask AI, answered by a tool or model you choose
- Thaw 3’s actions in the search
- Settings export and import

</RoadmapGroup>
</RoadmapList>

</Roadmap>

To ask for something, [open an issue](https://github.com/thaw-app/Floe/issues). Every request is considered during the early releases.
