Thaw is available in 20 languages. Translations are managed on Crowdin, not in git.

## Translate Thaw

1. Open [Thaw on Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/thaw) and sign in.
2. Pick your language. If it is not listed, request it there.
3. Translate or review strings in the browser. Crowdin shows how complete each language is.

<Callout type="info" title="No pull requests for translations">
  Translation pull requests are not accepted in the Thaw repository. Changes made on Crowdin reach the app through the project's own sync.
</Callout>

## Credit

Everyone who has translated Thaw is named in the project's [credits](https://github.com/thaw-app/Thaw/blob/development/CREDITS.md).
